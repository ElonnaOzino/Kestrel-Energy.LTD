Services

From first survey to steady production, our engineers support every stage of your project.

Exploration and production

We help operators find, evaluate and develop hydrocarbon resources with technical rigour.

  • Seismic data interpretation
  • Reservoir modelling and reserves estimation
  • Field development planning
  • Production optimisation

Drilling and well engineering

Safe, cost-effective wells designed and supervised by experienced drilling engineers.

  • Well planning and design
  • Rig and contractor supervision
  • Completions and workovers
  • Drilling fluids and well control

Pipelines and midstream

Moving product reliably from field to terminal, with integrity at every step.

  • Route survey and pipeline design
  • Construction management
  • Corrosion and integrity monitoring
  • Storage and terminal operations

Energy consulting

Independent advice that helps you make confident investment and compliance decisions.

  • Feasibility and economic studies
  • Health, safety and environment audits
  • Regulatory and permit support
  • Local content planning

Frequently asked questions

Which regions do you work in?

We operate onshore and offshore across West Africa, with a base in Port Harcourt.

Can you support a single phase of a project?

Yes. You can hire us for one stage, such as well design, or for the full project lifecycle.

How do you manage safety?

Every project follows a documented HSE plan, daily toolbox talks and independent audits.

We reply to enquiries within two working days and can mobilise a team in weeks.
